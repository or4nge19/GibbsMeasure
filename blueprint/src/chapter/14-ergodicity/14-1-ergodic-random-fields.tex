\section{Ergodic random fields}

Georgii opens Chapter 14 by observing that the convex geometry of the shift-invariant random
fields is an instance of the abstract kernel machinery of Section 7.1: the shift group acts by
the deterministic kernels $\hat\theta_i(A \mid \omega) = \1_A(\theta_i\omega)$ of (14.4), the
invariant fields $\mathcal{P}_{\Theta}$ are the $\Pi$-invariant measures of Corollary (7.4), and
the almost surely invariant events $\mathcal{I}(\mu)$ of (14.3) are $\mathcal{I}_{\Pi}(\mu)$.
The formalization takes the same route, at the generality of an arbitrary group action.

\begin{definition}[Invariant fields and invariant events, Georgii (14.1)--(14.4)]
    \label{def:erg-invariant}
    \uses{def:ext-ae-invariant}
    \lean{ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.smulKernel,
      ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.smulInvariantMeasure_iff_forall_invariant,
      ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.measurableSet_aeInvariantSigmaAlgebraFamily_smul}
    \leanok

    For a group $G$ acting measurably on $\Omega$, the kernel of the action of $g$ is
    `Kernel.deterministic (g • ·)` — Georgii's $\hat\theta_i$ — and Mathlib's
    \texttt{SMulInvariantMeasure} is exactly invariance under every such kernel. The
    $\mu$-almost surely invariant σ-algebra of the family is Georgii's $\mathcal{I}(\mu)$: the
    events whose preimage under every $g \cdot{}$ agrees with them $\mu$-a.e.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ergodicity is extremality, Georgii (14.5)(a) and (14.6)]
    \label{thm:erg-145a}
    \uses{def:erg-invariant, thm:ext-74}
    \lean{ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.ergodicSMul_iff_mem_extremePoints}
    \leanok

    An invariant probability measure is \emph{ergodic} — Mathlib's \texttt{ErgodicSMul}, i.e.
    trivial on $\mathcal{I}(\mu)$, which is Georgii's Definition (14.6) — if and only if it is an
    extreme point of the convex set of invariant probability measures. This is Corollary (7.4)
    applied to the kernels of (14.4), exactly as Georgii says; no countability of the group is
    needed. Mathlib's \texttt{ErgodicSMul} is already phrased with almost sure invariance, so no
    strict-to-a.e.\ correction arises, unlike the single-map case
    (Proposition~\ref{prop:ext-mathlib}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Parts (b), (c) and the ergodic decomposition]
    \label{rmk:erg-145bc}
    \uses{thm:erg-145a, thm:ext-invariant-density}

    (14.5)(b) — an absolutely continuous $\nu = f\mu$ is invariant iff $f$ is
    $\mathcal{I}(\mu)$-measurable — is Theorem~\ref{thm:ext-invariant-density} at the kernels of
    (14.4). The shift-specific development ((14.7) ergodicity as cube-averaged mixing, (14.10)
    the ergodic decomposition of $\mathcal{P}_{\Theta}$, (14.12) density of the extreme boundary)
    is not yet formalized; the general decomposition machinery it instantiates is Chapter 7's.
\end{remark}
